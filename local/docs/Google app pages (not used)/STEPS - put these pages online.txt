PUT THE APP'S WEB PAGES ONLINE (so Google lets you click "Publish app")
=====================================================================

Google won't publish the app until the Branding page has a home page and
privacy policy link. This folder has three ready-made pages:
  index.html    (home page)
  privacy.html  (privacy policy)
  terms.html    (terms of service)
They're short and honest: a private family tool that only touches files
it created in Drive. You'll put them online for free with GitHub Pages.


STEP 0: QUICK CHECK FIRST (30 seconds)
--------------------------------------
On the Google Audience page, press F5 to reload. Google sometimes doesn't
notice the Branding page was saved. If "Publish app" can now be clicked,
click it, confirm, and skip everything below.


STEP 1: MAKE A FREE GITHUB ACCOUNT (about 3 minutes)
----------------------------------------------------
1. Go to github.com and click "Sign up".
2. Choose a username. It becomes part of the web address and is public,
   so DON'T use your child's name. Something like "spo-family" is fine.
   Below, YOURNAME means the username you picked.


STEP 2: PUT THE PAGES ONLINE (about 3 minutes)
----------------------------------------------
1. On GitHub, click the "+" at the top right, then "New repository".
2. Repository name, typed exactly:   YOURNAME.github.io
   (for example:  spo-family.github.io)
   Leave it Public. Tick "Add a README file". Click "Create repository".
3. Click "Add file", then "Upload files".
   Drag in index.html, privacy.html and terms.html from this folder.
   (Don't upload this STEPS file.) Click "Commit changes".
4. Wait 1-2 minutes, then open  https://YOURNAME.github.io  in a new tab.
   You should see "School Paper Organizer".
   If you get a 404 page, wait another minute and reload. If it's still
   404: in the repository click "Settings", then "Pages", set "Source"
   to "Deploy from a branch", choose "main" and "/ (root)", click Save,
   and wait 2 minutes.


STEP 3: FILL IN GOOGLE'S BRANDING PAGE (about 2 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------
Go back to console.cloud.google.com, then Google Auth Platform, then Branding.
1. Under "Authorized domains", click "Add domain" and type:
       YOURNAME.github.io
2. Fill in:
   Application home page:          https://YOURNAME.github.io/
   Application privacy policy link: https://YOURNAME.github.io/privacy.html
   Application terms of service link: https://YOURNAME.github.io/terms.html
3. Still leave the LOGO blank.
4. Click Save at the bottom.


STEP 4: PUBLISH
---------------
1. Click "Audience" on the left.
2. Click "Publish app", then "Confirm".
   The status should change to "In production".
   If Google offers to "verify" the app, you don't need to. Skip it.
3. Go back to the main setup file, Part 2, step 4 (create the Desktop
   client and download the JSON).

If Google shows an error at any step, take a screenshot
(Win + Shift + S) and paste it to Claude with Ctrl + V.
