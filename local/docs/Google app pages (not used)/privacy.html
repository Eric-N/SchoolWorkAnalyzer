Privacy Policy

School Paper Organizer is a private tool used only by one family on its own computer.

What it accesses

With the user's permission, it uses the Google Drive "drive.file" scope. This lets it create files and folders in the user's Google Drive and update the files it created. It cannot see or change any other files in the user's Drive.

How data is used

Scanned papers are processed only on the family's own computer. The results are uploaded to the family's own Google Drive. No data is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
